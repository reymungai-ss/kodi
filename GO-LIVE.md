# Kodi: go-live note for Baraka Court

**What it does.** Kodi answers your tenants on WhatsApp, day or night. A
tenant signs in with their house number and a PIN. Kodi then tells them what
they owe, sends a payment link (M-Pesa or card), and marks the rent paid only
once Paystack confirms the money has arrived. It can agree a two-part plan
within your rule: half now, the rest by the 20th. You and John can ask it
"who hasn't paid?" at any time. Disputes, repairs and anything unusual become
a ticket for John.

**What it doesn't do.** It can't refund, waive, discount or move money out,
and it never asks for M-Pesa PINs or card numbers. It doesn't chase anyone:
tenants message it first. It only knows what's in the rent sheet.

**My recommendation: start small.** Run Kodi on Baraka Court for one month
alongside your current method, with John approving every payment plan for the
first two weeks. If tenants use it and keep their plans, let it run on its
own. If most ignore it, keep the receipts and rent sheet and drop the plans.

**Before tenants use it:**
1. **Live payments.** Paystack verifies your business, then Kodi switches to
   live keys, kept only on the server. Kodi refuses live keys today on
   purpose. Finish with one real test payment.
2. **Kodi's own WhatsApp number,** introduced to tenants by John, not by a
   surprise message. John gives each tenant their PIN in person. Anyone who
   forgets it, or is locked out after five wrong tries, goes to John. PINs
   show in Kodi's chat history, so keep that and the sheet private.
3. **Alerts.** John gets a WhatsApp or SMS for every ticket and locked
   account; whoever maintains Kodi gets one if it stops responding.
4. **Payments nobody mentions.** If a tenant pays but never tells Kodi, the
   sheet waits until someone checks. Add automatic confirmation from Paystack
   before launch, and compare the sheet with your Paystack statement monthly.
5. **Monthly routine.** John enters each month's rent and any arrears by the
   1st. Keep a weekly backup of the sheet.
6. **Tenants without WhatsApp.** About half of Kenyans don't use it. They keep
   paying the usual way until Kodi has an SMS version.
