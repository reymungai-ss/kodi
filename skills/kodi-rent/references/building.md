# Baraka Court: how the building runs

- **Building:** Baraka Court, 6 flats (A1–A3, B1–B3) over three floors. B3 is vacant.
- **Rent** is due on the 5th of each month. Amounts differ by flat (KES 18,000–25,000).
- **Arrears** from earlier months are carried into the current month's balance.
- **Paying:** only through the Paystack link Kodi sends (M-Pesa or card). The
  building does not accept cash through Kodi, and staff will never ask for an M-Pesa PIN.
- **Payment plans:** a tenant may split one month into two payments: at least
  half now, the rest by the 20th. One plan at a time.
- **Landlord:** Mr. Kamau. **Caretaker:** Joseph Njoroge (on site; handles
  repairs, keys, water and security).
- **PINs:** each tenant gets a 4-digit PIN from the caretaker to sign in to
  Kodi with their house number. The caretaker can reset a forgotten or locked PIN.
- **Office hours** for human follow-up: Monday–Saturday, 8am–6pm.
- This is a test setup. Payments run in Paystack test mode with made-up tenants.
